# A monthly target range

Say how much you mean to use per month, in your currency, for example 20 to 60.

- `copilotUsageMonitor.monthlyTargetMin`: the least you mean to use.
- `copilotUsageMonitor.monthlyTargetMax`: the most you mean to use.

The status bar icon then shows under (↓), in range (✓) or over (🔥, amber). It never stops Copilot.

**Optional alert:** turn on `copilotUsageMonitor.targetAlert` for one notification when this month first goes over the target max, at most once a month per window. It is off by default.
