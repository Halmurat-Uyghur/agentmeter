# The side bar and the panel

- **Side bar:** the AgentMeter logo in the activity bar, "AgentMeter (Local)". Today, the last 7 days and this month, plus top projects and models.
- **Panel:** Overview, Sessions, Daily, Models, Projects, Tools, Insights, Data health and Settings. Projects, Models and Tools show the change against the same point last month.

Click the status bar item to open the panel at any time.
