# The side bar and the panel

- **Side bar:** the graph icon in the activity bar, "Copilot Usage (Local)". Today, the last 7 days and this month, plus top projects and models.
- **Panel:** Overview, Daily, Sessions, Models, Projects, Tools, Insights and Data health. Projects, Models and Tools show the change against the same point last month.

Click the status bar item to open the panel at any time.
